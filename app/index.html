    Roadmap to the Platinum — 200–250 hours (up to 300+)

    One playthrough is enough — no mission replay, no difficulty setting; free roam continues after the story and only one meditation can be missed.

    Platinum: ~200 h (PowerPyx, OutputLag) · 200–250 h (damnmodz) · up to 300+ h (Push Square). Stages: story 60–80 h · checklist ~100 h · clean-up ~10 h.

    Missable trophies

    Only one trophy may be missable. Everything else can be done after the story in free roam — there is no mission replay, but nothing else is locked away.

    What is NOT missable

    • All 12 story trophies — they unlock automatically and cannot be missed.
    • All 5 side-character final missions (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone) — quest givers return after the story.
    • Strangers & Freaks — all stranger strands stay available in free roam, EXCEPT Race Against Yago in Chapter 3: it is timed and can expire, so do it as soon as it appears.
    • Every robbery, vault, gang operation, race, range and collectible — all doable after the credits.
    • Optional missions that disappear mid-story do not matter: there are 200–300+, and the 100% checklist only needs 100.

    Cheap insurance: keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter (15 manual slots). Sources: PowerPyx; GTABase.
